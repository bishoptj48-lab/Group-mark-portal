Privacy & Cryptographic Security

This portal uses zero-knowledge client-side SHA-256 cryptographic hashing.

The results.json file contains only irreversible SHA-256 cryptographic fingerprints. Your actual student registration number and name are never stored on GitHub or transmitted unhashed over the internet. When you type your number, your device performs the calculation locally and looks up only your matching record.
Make sure you entered your registration number exactly as registered (e.g. VU-CSF-2603-1632). Spacing and letter capitalization are handled automatically. If still not found, contact your group leader or lecturer to ensure your group data has been exported to the website.
A score of "Awaiting grading" means your group leader has submitted the preliminary evaluation, but the lecturer has not yet finalized their grade review. Once finalized, the lecturer re-exports results.json to update this site.
